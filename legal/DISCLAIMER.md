# Disclaimer and acceptable use

Null-Trace is a defensive research tool. It collects information that is already
public, or that you are authorised to look at.

## You may use it for

- security testing of systems you own or have written permission to test;
- incident response and fraud investigation inside your organisation;
- checking your own exposure — accounts, domains, numbers, devices;
- research and journalism on public figures and public organisations, within
  your jurisdiction's law.

## You may not use it for

- scanning, probing or enumerating systems without authorisation;
- building profiles of private individuals, including looking up relatives,
  family members, home addresses or unlisted contact details;
- harassment, stalking, doxxing or intimidation;
- bypassing authentication, paywalls or access controls;
- collecting or redistributing personal data outside a lawful basis.

## Data you will encounter

Breach records and infostealer logs contain personal data that was never meant
to be public. Handling them is regulated — GDPR in the EU, DPDP Act 2023 in
India, CCPA in California, and equivalents elsewhere. A finding is evidence of
exposure; it is not a licence to use the data.

Possession of material describing abuse, or of credentials belonging to others,
may itself be a criminal offence regardless of how it was obtained.
